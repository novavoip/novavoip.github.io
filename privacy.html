Nova Voice Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

At Nova Voice ("NV", "we", "our", or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use the Nova Voice service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with our platform, as well as automatically through your use of Nova Voice:

  • Account Information: When you register for an account, we collect your chosen username, password hash, and valid email address.
  • User Audio & Transcripts: Voice recordings, audio samples, or text data generated or uploaded to Nova Voice to facilitate voice services.
  • Log & Technical Data: System information such as IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, and crash reports collected automatically to maintain system performance and security.
  • Cookies & Local Storage: We use local storage and session cookies exclusively to keep you authenticated and preserve user preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or sell user profiles to advertisers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining the Nova Voice platform;
  • Processing voice data and delivering audio synth/transcription functionality;
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service and community safety guidelines (e.g., investigating reports of prohibited behavior or ban evasion);
  • Communicating critical service updates, technical notices, or security alerts;
  • Troubleshooting bugs and preventing security incidents or legal breaches.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data under any circumstances. We only share information in the following limited situations:

  • Compliance with Law: When required by a valid subpoena, court order, lawsuit, DMCA notice, governmental demand under applicable law, or when disclosing necessary information to appropriate legal authorities.
  • Safety & Terms Enforcement: If necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nova Voice, our users, or the public.
  • Infrastructure Providers: With trusted server hosting and network service providers who process data strictly under our instruction and subject to confidentiality agreements.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Account data is retained for as long as your account remains active. Temporary voice processing cache files are deleted automatically after processing, unless explicitly saved to your account library. You may request account deletion at any time to purge your personal data from active databases, subject to Section 7.

5. Children's Privacy

Nova Voice is strictly intended for users who are at least 13 years of age (or the legal minimum age in your jurisdiction) to comply with COPPA regulations. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. Authorized moderators may review voice data within the platform for moderation purposes, but are strictly prohibited from exporting or uploading this data off-site. If we discover that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a child under 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will take immediate action to terminate the account and delete associated data.

6. Educational (Edu) Accounts

For specialized Edu Accounts managed by educators, student data collected within designated classroom environments is processed strictly for educational purposes and subjected to heightened privacy standards. Edu account data is never used for commercial profiling.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Access & Export: Requesting a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Updating or correcting your username or email address through account settings.
  • Deletion & Denials: Requesting complete account closure and removal of your personal data. We reserve the right to deny deletion requests if an account is under investigation for severe Terms of Service violations or illegal activity. In such events, account data will be preserved and shared with appropriate law enforcement authorities as required by law.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our technology, legal requirements, or operations. When updates occur, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of Nova Voice after any updates signifies your agreement to the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact the Nova Voice development team.
